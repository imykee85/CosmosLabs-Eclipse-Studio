CREATOR CHANNEL CRAFT

Eclipse notes in our own words, written after reading two short "AI filmmaking career" skills the owner uploaded (building a channel, and selling templates); the originals are kept unchanged in docs/prompt-engines/knowledge/original/creator-channel/. Their income figures, price lists and platform fees are NOT used: they are unverified and change. Use this when the person wants to build an audience with AI-made video, run a channel with a recurring character, produce in batches, or package what they know into something to sell.

1. A RECURRING HOST
One consistent character across every video builds familiarity. Make the character once (a locked description and, where the model allows, a saved Soul ID), keep the same wording in every prompt, and keep the same voice. Everything the character says or does should fit their one personality.

2. WHAT TO MAKE
A series with the same character and different stories; a "made in a day" process video; mood pieces; before-and-after breakdowns of technique; your own take on a familiar scene. Pick one lane and stay in it long enough to be recognised.

3. PRODUCE IN BATCHES
Plan five episodes in one session, make all the visuals in one session, edit all of them in one session, then schedule them across a few weeks. Never make one video at a time. In Eclipse: write the scripts with Treatment or Atlas, make the shots in Image Studio and Video Studio with the character as an Ingredient, keep the sound in Audio Studio, and use a Canvas for a repeatable chain.

4. SHORT CLIPS FEED THE CHANNEL
Post the same material as short vertical clips on other platforms and point each back to the channel. Treat them as one system, not separate silos.

5. SELLING WHAT YOU KNOW
- Possible products: prompt packs, workflow guides for one use case, script templates by genre, editing presets. Start with ONE product, not a shop. Put the link where your audience already is. If it sells, make another; if not, change the positioning and try again.
- Tell buyers exactly what is inside and what it does not do. Never promise income, views or results, and do not copy other people's templates or sell prompts that reproduce a protected character or a real person.
- A free spec sample to show your work is fine. Ongoing free work is not: if the sample is liked, quote a real price.

6. HONESTY
Say what is AI-made where a platform or the law asks for it, check the rules for earning money on each platform, and treat all earnings as uncertain. Do not quote figures you cannot support.

7. YOUR DELIVERY RULES (Atlas)
- Give a plan for the next batch (five titles, one character line, one posting rhythm) before any strategy talk.
