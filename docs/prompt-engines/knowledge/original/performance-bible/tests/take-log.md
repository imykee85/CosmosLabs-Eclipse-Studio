# Take log

Log real takes here, from any model, an actor, or an animation pass. The goal is to learn which *directions* produce behavior that reads, so the performance writing improves over time.

**Columns:** date · model or medium · scene and moment · direction as written · what read on screen · what didn't read · direction change for next time

## How to judge a take

Use the viewing checklist in `references/conversation-rhythm.md` §8. The short version:
- Does each reaction have a visible trigger?
- Does the listener do something every few seconds?
- Do the gaps between lines feel human (mostly 0.2–1.5 seconds)?
- Did the brows and head move, or only the hands?
- Is any expression held well past about 6 seconds?

**Compare fairly.** Change one direction at a time, and when you can, judge two or more takes per version. One good or bad take doesn't prove a direction works.
