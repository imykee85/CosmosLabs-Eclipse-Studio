# Worked example: one character, three traits, four real places

**Setup given to the skill:**
- A character sheet: a man in his early thirties with rimless gold glasses, layered gold chains, a washed-black crewneck and tan suede boots.
- Three traits: **"confident, extroverted, open."**
- The ask: put him in notable places in Los Angeles and New York.

Everything else below was inferred by the skill.

The output is **performance directions only**. Pair them with your own reference images, camera and settings in whatever model or medium you use.

"Extroverted" pulled in The Performer, and with it a fear of being ordinary that wasn't asked for. A secure-extravert archetype ("Host" or "Connector") would fit people like this better. It's on the to-do list.

## 2. Cast card

**Theo, 31** (working name; swap freely)
- **Look:** per the character sheet.
- **Archetype:** The Performer, with a Steady One floor. He recovers fast and never sulks.
- **Origin:** a Brooklyn big-city kid from a loud house. His gestures are sharp and punctuating, he's comfortable up close, he talks over people, and he reaches across counters.
- **Fear:** being ordinary.
- **Want across the scenes:** to be the one who made it out and still belongs on the block.
- **Secret:** he's thinking about moving home.
- **Baseline body:** full belly breath, open posture, weight settled, takes up space. Holds eye contact with eyebrows active. Big gestures from the shoulder.
- **Under pressure:** gets louder, jokes, touches the other person's arm or shoulder.
- **Hiding tell:** holds eye contact one beat too long, and tells a longer story than he was asked for.
- **Voice:** warm mid-low register, quick Brooklyn cadence, laughs in the middle of his own lines.

**Through-line:** in LA he's selling the city; in New York he lingers. The secret should read without anyone saying it.

## 3. Scenes

### Scene 1: LA · Grand Central Market, a taco counter · midday
- **Partner:** Lupe, 50s, runs the counter. **The Fixer:** wipes, sorts tickets, keeps the line moving.
- **Scene engine:**
  - He's a regular; she has never once smiled at him.
  - **Objectives:** his is to get her to laugh before she hands over the plate. Hers is to keep the line moving.
  - **Register:** naturalistic.
  - **Status:** Lupe → Theo, flipping when she has to hide a smile.

| # | Trigger | Underneath | Theo | Lupe (listener) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Her back is turned | His running project | Leans on his forearms, brows up, delivers the line | Keeps wiping, doesn't look up |
| 2 | He says "smile" | It landed, and she won't show it | Waits, grinning | Presses her lips together, a slight head tilt, eyes still on the tickets |
| 3 | The plate is ready | She ends the exchange | Watches her | Slides the tacos over: "Next." |
| 4 | She turns away | She lost | Raises his brows, slaps the counter, laughs | The corner of her mouth starts to lift, and she hides it by facing the grill |

**Direction:** Theo leans both forearms on the steel counter, weight forward, eyebrows raised, eyes on the woman behind it. He says, quick and warm, laughing halfway through the line: "Lupe, I'm telling you, one day you're gonna smile at me." Lupe keeps wiping the steel in short hard strokes and reads the tickets above the grill. She doesn't speak or look up while he talks. When he says "smile," she presses her lips together and tilts her head slightly, still not looking at him. After a short beat, she slides a paper boat of three tacos across to him and says flatly, eyes on the tickets: "Next." As she turns back to the grill, the corner of her mouth starts to lift, and she hides it by facing the grill. He raises his eyebrows, slaps the counter once with his palm, and laughs.

### Scene 2: LA · Griffith Observatory terrace · late afternoon
- **Partner:** Jalen, 22, his cousin visiting from Brooklyn. **The Fortress:** hands in his hoodie pocket, says little.
- **Scene engine:**
  - **Objectives:** Theo's is to get Jalen to say he'd move out here. Jalen's is not to commit.
  - **Underneath:** Theo is homesick.
  - **Register:** naturalistic, with one loaded gap.
  - **Status:** Theo → Jalen, flipping on one quiet question.

| # | Trigger | Underneath | Theo | Jalen (listener) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The view | Host showing off | Sweeps his arm across the skyline: "All of that. Twenty minutes from my place." | Watches the city over Theo's shoulder, not speaking |
| 2 | Theo's pitch | He's not buying it | Waits for a reaction | After a short beat, turns his head, looks straight at him: "You miss home?" |
| 3 | The question | Caught | Arm lowers to the parapet. About four seconds of silence, holding Jalen's eyes one beat too long | Holds the look |
| 4 | The silence | Covering | Breaks into a wide smile, claps Jalen's shoulder twice | Lets him |

**Direction:** The two men stand at the white parapet facing each other in profile, the city spread out between them. Theo sweeps his arm across the skyline, rings catching the light, and says, quick and warm: "All of that. Twenty minutes from my place." Jalen keeps his hands in his hoodie pocket and watches the city over Theo's shoulder, not speaking while Theo talks. After a short beat, Jalen turns his head, looks straight at him, and asks quietly: "You miss home?" Theo's arm lowers to the parapet. For about four seconds, neither speaks. Theo keeps his eyes on Jalen's without blinking, jaw still. Then he breaks into a wide smile and claps Jalen's shoulder twice.

### Scene 3: NYC · Katz's Delicatessen cutting counter · lunch rush
- **Partner:** the cutter, 60s. **The Judge:** evaluates first and gives nothing away.
- **Scene engine:**
  - **Objectives:** Theo's is to prove he's still from here and earn the thick cut. The cutter's is to make him earn it.
  - **Register:** heightened; it's a busy counter.
  - **Status:** cutter → Theo, flipping when the cutter adds a second slice. That's a prop event, not a nod.

| # | Trigger | Underneath | Theo | Cutter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theo steps up | Testing him | Watches the knife | Carves one thick slice, lays it on the empty plate, slides it across, eyes on Theo's face |
| 2 | The taste | Home | Eats it in one bite, eyes close, head tips back | Watching, not smiling |
| 3 | The reaction | Honest praise | Points at him: "Ten years in LA, and nobody does it like this." | Silent, holding his look |
| 4 | That line | Approval, unspoken | Drops a folded bill into the tip cup | Carves a second, thicker slice, drops it on Theo's plate, pushes it back |

**Direction:** The cutting board is bare except for one steaming slab and a single empty plate. The cutter carves one thick slice, lays it on the plate, and slides it across to Theo, watching his face without a word. Theo eats it in one bite, closes his eyes and tips his head back, chewing slowly. Then he points at the cutter and says, fast and warm, laughing: "Ten years in LA, and nobody does it like this." The cutter doesn't smile and doesn't speak. He carves a second, thicker slice, drops it onto Theo's plate, and pushes the plate back toward him. Theo drops a folded bill into the tip cup.

### Scene 4: NYC · DUMBO, Washington Street · overcast morning
- **Partner:** Nia, 31, a childhood friend. **The Steady One:** calm, and sees straight through him.
- **Scene engine:**
  - **Objectives:** Theo's is to sell her on LA, and himself. Nia's is to get him to admit why he keeps flying back.
  - **Register:** naturalistic.
  - **Status:** Theo → Nia, flipping when she stops walking and he has to turn back to her.

| # | Trigger | Underneath | Theo | Nia (listener) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The bridge behind them | Selling | Big hands, points back at the bridge: "I'm just saying, the light out there is different." | Walks at an even pace, nods once; at "different," her brows go up |
| 2 | His pitch | She's heard this before | Keeps walking | Stops |
| 3 | She's gone from beside him | Exposed | Takes two more steps before he notices, then turns back | Waits |
| 4 | His turn | She names it | Listens | After a short beat, tilts her head: "Then why do you keep coming back?" |
| 5 | The question | Deflecting | Laughs too loud, steps back to her, touches her elbow, walks on, steering her with him | Lets herself be steered, looking at him |

**Direction:** They walk side by side down the cobblestones, the bridge behind them. Theo talks with big hands, turns to point back at the bridge, and says, quick and warm: "I'm just saying, the light out there is different." Nia walks at an even pace with a paper coffee cup, not speaking while he talks. She nods once, and on "different" her eyebrows go up. Then she stops walking. He takes two more steps before he notices, then turns back to face her. After a short beat, she tilts her head and says calmly: "Then why do you keep coming back?" He laughs, a beat too loud, steps back to her, touches her elbow, and starts walking again, steering her along with him.
