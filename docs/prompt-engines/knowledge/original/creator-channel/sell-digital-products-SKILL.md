# Sell Templates & Digital Products (Path C)

**KPI:** Turn process knowledge into products that earn money while you sleep.

## What you can sell
| Product | What it is | Price |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Prompt packs | Best Claude prompts for scripting, shot planning, 7ART generation (20–50 prompts, PDF/Notion) | $9–$29 |
| CapCut editing presets | Transition styles, color adjustments, text templates as downloadable presets | $7–$19 |
| Workflow guides | Step-by-step PDF/video of your exact process, specific to one use case (AI product ads, RE tours) | $19–$49 |
| Script templates | Claude prompt templates per genre (horror trailer, product commercial, travel vlog, RE tour) | $9–$19 / pack |

## Where to sell
| Platform | Best for | Fee |
|----------|----------|-----|
| Gumroad | Digital downloads (PDFs, templates) | 10% |
| Payhip | Same as Gumroad, slightly lower fees | 5% |
| Etsy | Digital products with search traffic | 6.5% + listing |
| Your own website | Full control, no platform fees | Hosting only |

## How to start
Build **one** product — not a store. List it on Gumroad, put the link in your YouTube description and social bios. If it sells, make more; if it doesn't, adjust the positioning and try again.

## Objection: "Should I offer the first video for free?"
- **Do** create a spec sample without being asked — that's a legitimate portfolio piece.
- **Don't** offer ongoing free work. If they like the sample, quote a real price.
- Free sets a precedent that's very hard to undo. A low price is fine; free is a trap.

## Rules
- Ship one product first; validate before expanding.
- The link lives in your existing channels (YouTube desc, bios) — no cold store.
- Spec samples yes, ongoing free work no.
