# YouTube & Content Creation (Path B)

**KPI:** Grow an audience with AI filmmaking and convert it into ad, sponsorship, and product revenue.

## Your AI character = recurring host
The character you trained with SoulID becomes the face of the channel — same face every video, no camera. Builds audience familiarity without you ever appearing on screen.

## Content ideas that work
- AI short films in a series (same character, different stories)
- "Made in 24 hours" challenge videos showing your process
- Cinematic mood pieces set to trending music
- Before-and-after breakdowns of your filmmaking techniques
- AI recreations of famous movie scenes in your own style

## Batch production workflow (1 day → 2–3 weeks of content)
1. Script 5 episodes with Claude in one session (~1 hr)
2. Generate all visuals in one 7ART session (~2–3 hrs)
3. Edit all 5 in CapCut (~3–4 hrs)
4. Schedule uploads across 2–3 weeks

## Monetization milestones
| Milestone | Revenue source | Typical range |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| 0–1,000 subs | Build portfolio | No direct revenue |
| 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hrs | YouTube Partner Program (ads) | $1–$5 / 1k views |
| 5,000+ subs | Brand sponsorships | $200–$1,000 / video |
| 10,000+ subs | Digital products, courses, templates | $500–$5,000 / mo |

- YPP threshold typically takes 3–6 months of weekly uploads.
- Shorts accelerate this hard — one viral Short can deliver thousands of subs overnight.

## TikTok as accelerator
Post the same AI content as TikToks and Reels to drive traffic to YouTube. TikTok's algorithm pushes high-quality AI visuals aggressively (they generate comments, shares, saves). Every TikTok description points back: "Full film on YouTube — link in bio."

## Rules
- One consistent AI character across all content — familiarity compounds.
- Batch-produce; never create one video at a time.
- Cross-post to TikTok/Reels to feed the YouTube channel, not as a separate silo.
